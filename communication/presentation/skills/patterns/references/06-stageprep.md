# Stage Prep

_Readying yourself and the room in the minutes and hours before you speak._

**Preparation** (P) — Murphy's Law applies; eliminate variables so a disaster becomes a nuisance. How: work a checklist — supported projector resolutions, display dongles, presenter remote(s), topic notes, water, audience-management techniques; pack laptop bag night before with duplicate power bricks/USB cables; back up deck to 2+ locations (USB, drive, cloud); dress comfortably but appropriately for audience; hydrate (add electrolytes for all-day). See: Know Your Audience, Carnegie Hall.
**Seeding Satisfaction** (P) — Be your own warm-up act: greet/prime audience before start so you're not a stranger and one friendly face carries the room. How: arrive early, shake hands, ask sincere questions; gather live data on skill level/interest to tailor emphasis; build rapport, hand out materials, redirect people in the wrong talk. See: Know Your Audience.
**Posse** (P) — Plant friends/team in audience as focal smiling faces to reduce your stress and prime strangers to rate you positively. How: seat supporters front/center, seed them with questions, have them clap/nod, use them as assistants if equipment fails; favor early events that let you bring a colleague; frame travel cost as marketing. Avoid: don't fake stunts (planted laughs/questions) — sensed rigging causes backlash. See: Greek Chorus.
**Display of High Value** (P) — Audience confers high status expecting you bring new knowledge; adopt the mantle to serve them, calming both sides. How: internally accept the status differential; don't apologize for prep/AV problems — move on; answer questions concisely and end debate early (offer 1-on-1 after); acknowledge questions you can't answer and defer follow-up. Avoid: Display of Low Value (contrived humility insults audience); and abusing audience as props to feign strength. See: Carnegie Hall, Know Your Audience, Going Meta, Hecklers.
**Shortchanged** (AP) — Last-minute time cut (previous speaker runs over); poorly handled you rush, cut key material, deliver less. Fix: don't panic; politely but firmly reclaim your slot (hover by exit, approach front, help them pack, escalate to organizer); skip Seeding Satisfaction, deploy Expansion Joints or drop a section; know in advance which slides are cut-able and skip them gracefully via keyboard shortcuts (never zoom past visibly). Avoid: Going Meta explaining the late start. See: Preparation, Expansion Joints, Display of High Value.
