# The decision, stated as a claim

**Context.** The situation that forced a choice: the constraint, the problem, what was true when
the decision was made.

**Decision.** What was chosen, concretely enough to act on.

**Consequences.** What this buys and what it costs, including the trade-off accepted and anything
downstream it forces.

**Set aside.** The alternative that was considered and dropped, and why, so it is not re-proposed
without new information. If there was no real alternative, say that explicitly rather than omitting
the section.
