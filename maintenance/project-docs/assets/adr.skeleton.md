# The decision, stated as a claim

**Context.** The situation that forced a choice: the constraint, the problem, what was true when
the decision was made.

**Decision.** What was chosen, concretely enough to act on [^alt].

**Consequences.** What this buys and what it costs, including the trade-off accepted and anything
downstream it forces.

[^alt]:
    The alternative that was considered and dropped, and why, so it is not re-proposed without
    new information. A denied or discarded alternative lives here as a footnote on the active
    decision, not as its own file. Drop this footnote when there was no real alternative.
