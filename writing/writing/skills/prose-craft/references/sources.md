# Sources

## Primary

- Strunk, William Jr. _The Elements of Style_ (1918). Public domain; the source for the 1918
  rule numbers used in `checklist.md` and `canon.md`.
  - Bartleby full text: https://www.bartleby.com/141/
  - Project Gutenberg (#37134): https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/37134
- Strunk, William Jr. and E. B. White. _The Elements of Style_, 4th ed. (Longman, 2000). The
  source for "use definite, specific, concrete language" and the "leeches" line, both White
  additions absent from the 1918 text.
  - Overview: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elements_of_Style
- Pinker, Steven. _The Sense of Style: The Thinking Person's Guide to Writing in the 21st
  Century_ (Viking, 2014). Classic style (ch. 2), the curse of knowledge (ch. 3), the tree and
  arcs of coherence (ch. 4).
  - Notes and summary: https://sive.rs/book/SenseOfStyle
  - "The Curse of Knowledge" (APS): https://www.psychologicalscience.org/observer/the-curse-of-knowledge-pinker-describes-a-key-cause-of-bad-writing
- Pinker, Steven. "Why Academics Stink at Writing" (_Chronicle of Higher Education_, 2014).
  - PDF: https://stevenpinker.com/files/pinker/files/why_academics_stink_at_writing.pdf

## Convergent style guides

Used for the universals in `SKILL.md` (active voice, short words, lead with the answer,
sentence-case headings, serial comma) and for the meta-rule that rules are defaults, not gates.

- Google developer documentation style guide: https://developers.google.com/style/highlights
- Microsoft Writing Style Guide, top 10 tips:
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/top-10-tips-style-voice
- The Economist Style Guide (Orwell's six rules, held loosely; "break any of these sooner than
  say anything barbarous"): https://cdn.static-economist.com/sites/default/files/pdfs/style_guide_12.pdf

## Voice matching

The optional voice-matching mode in `voice-matching.md` adapts the measure-edit-remeasure
method from the MIT-licensed `better-writing` skill: gate on a real user sample, read a
multi-axis voice profile including negative space, edit minimally, then re-measure and treat a
large axis shift as a voice break. Method re-derived and reworded; no text copied.

- better-writing (Forjd, MIT): https://github.com/forjd/better-writing

## Boundary

The line between composing well (this skill) and removing AI tells (the `unslop` skill) follows
the constructive-versus-subtractive split set out in `canon.md`. The tell inventory itself lives
in `unslop`, not here.
