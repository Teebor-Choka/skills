# Phrases to delete

Openers, closers, sycophancy, disclaimers, and filler that mark chatbot output. Delete on
sight unless a phrase carries real meaning in context. Apply the dose rule: a single
transition is fine, clustering is the failure.

## Throat-clearing openers

"Here's the thing:", "The uncomfortable truth is", "It turns out", "The real X is",
"Let me be clear", "The truth is,", "I'm going to be honest", "Can we talk about",
"Here's what I find interesting", "Here's the problem though", "Let's be real",
"Here's the deal:", "Here's what nobody tells you:", "It's no secret that", "Here's why",
"This is where it gets interesting", "Let's dive in / dive into", "Let's unpack",
"Let's explore / examine", "Let's break this down", "Let's take a look",
"Before we dive in", "Picture this", "Imagine a world where", "Have you ever wondered",
"Gone are the days", "Without further ado", "Look no further", "Chances are",
"You're not alone".

## Sycophancy and eager openers

"Great question!", "That's an excellent / thoughtful question", "Absolutely!",
"Of course!", "Certainly!", "Sure thing!", "No problem", "Happy to help",
"I'd be happy to", "You're absolutely right", "That's a great point",
"I couldn't agree more", "You make an excellent point", "I'm glad you asked".

## Closers, sign-offs, chatbot artifacts

"I hope this helps", "Hope this was helpful", "Let me know if you need anything else",
"Let me know if you have any questions", "Feel free to ask / reach out",
"Don't hesitate to", "Happy coding", "Best of luck", "Would you like me to...",
"I assure you", "as an AI assistant / language model", "as a large language model".

## Deference and self-referential compliance

"I am happy to address", "I am open to any suggestions / to feedback",
"I would appreciate any feedback", "I am willing to address", "demonstrate my
commitment", "with the utmost care", "If you have any concerns or suggestions".

## Knowledge-cutoff disclaimers

"as of my last update / knowledge cutoff", "based on my training data",
"based on available information", "I don't have access to real-time", "as of [date]"
(as a disclaimer), "while specific details are limited / scarce", "not widely
documented", "maintains a low profile".

## Didactic filler wrappers

"It's important / crucial / critical / essential to note / remember / consider",
"It's worth noting / mentioning", "It should be noted that", "Keep in mind that",
"One must remember", "Needless to say", "It goes without saying", "Results may vary".

## Meta-commentary and instruction narration

"Hint:", "Plot twist:", "Spoiler", "Pro tip", "Hot take", "Unpopular opinion",
"Let me explain", "To put it simply", "In other words", "If you think about it",
"As I mentioned / as mentioned above", "As we saw / as we'll see", "recall that",
"Per your request I kept this concise", "no files were changed", "I avoided em dashes as
instructed", "as requested I", "following your instructions".

## Reasoning-chain leaks

"Let me think step by step", "Breaking this down", "To approach this systematically",
"Here's my thought process", "Working through this logically".

## Filler phrases and verbose swaps

Delete: "At its core", "In today's X", "In a world where", "In an era of",
"The reality is", "In terms of", "With that said / That being said",
"All things considered", "By and large", "For the most part", "To be fair",
"To be honest", "The bottom line", "The key takeaway", "It's clear that",
"A closer look", "The fact that".

Shorten:

- "In order to" → "To"
- "Due to the fact that" → "Because"
- "Is able to / Has the ability to" → "Can"
- "At this point in time" → "Now"
- "In the event that" → "If"
- "Prior to" → "Before"
- "Subsequent to" → "After"
- "A large number of" → "Many"
- "The vast majority of" → "Most"
