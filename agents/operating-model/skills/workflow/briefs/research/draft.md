# Draft the note

Write the note from the claims and their verdicts. In a later round, also address every finding of the last
claim review.

The note has these sections, in this order:

1. `Question`: the restated question.
2. `Answer`: at most five sentences. Use only `verified` claims.
3. `Verified`: each verified claim with its citation (URL and access date).
4. `Hearsay`: each hearsay claim, marked as such, with its citation.
5. `Disagreements`: each pair of claims that disagree, with both sources.
6. `Not verified`: refuted and unreachable claims, and sub-questions with no source.

Every claim in the note keeps its citation. Do not add a claim that is not in the claim list.
