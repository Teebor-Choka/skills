# Rubric: claim review of the note

The judge reads the note, the claim list, the verdicts and the reviewer's findings. The note passes only
when every rule holds.

1. Every statement of fact in the note is a claim from the claim list, and it cites that claim's source
   with a URL and an access date.
2. No claim in the note says more than its cited quote says.
3. The `Answer` section uses only claims with the verdict `verified`.
4. Every claim with the verdict `hearsay` sits in the `Hearsay` section and is marked as hearsay.
5. Every `refuted` or `unreachable` claim, and every sub-question with no source, is listed under
   `Not verified`.
6. Every pair of claims that disagree is listed under `Disagreements` with both sources.
7. The reviewer's findings were all addressed, or the note says why one was not.

## On a fail

Report the number of each rule that fails, the claim id and the note section, and one sentence on what is
wrong. The next draft round receives this report.
